RV3R

Project Overview

Jane 'RVER' Doe has dedicated her life to finding a solution to moving objects at lightspeed. During the testing phase, the lightspeed engine goes haywire. the machine ends up collapsing in on itself, pulling in everything around it, Rover awakes sometime later to find herself on Mars, only to find a human society already there.

Genres: Isometric, 3D, 2D, Retro, Story, Single Player, Survival, JRPG, Souls-like, Exploration, Sci-fi
Engine: Unity
Team-Size: 2
Duration: On Hold
