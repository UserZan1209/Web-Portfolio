Regeneration

Project Overview

The theme for the competition was 'Regeneration' I came up with the idea of an electric bug that explores a forest finding and repairing generators. The player would lose health at a constant rate needing to use the generators to recharge. The enemies are also attracted to the generators but once draining them begin to damage them, this allows enemies to become supercharged.

Genres: GameJam, Top-down, illustration Graphics, 2D, Retro, Arcade, Single Player, Survival, Tower Defence, Exploration, Sci-fi
Engine: Unity
Team-Size: 2
Duration: Completed [one week]



Technical Challenges

This project came about during my summer break, I wanted to work on a small project to sharpen my skills. I decided to participate in a Game Jam.

The players goal is to repair generators to explore further away from spawn protecting generators, the player has attacking capabilities that also use the same energy meter for both the health and the stamina for attacking creating a sort of resource management aspect to the game.

This was the first time I participated in a Game Jam so having to design interesting game mechanics around a random theme made for an interesting and engaging design process rapidly prototyping mechanics you come up with to decide on something that's a good balance of being on theme whilst coming together to form a complete gameplay loop.

​ Time management is crucial during a games development even more so during such a short period to develop. This is the project that properly illustrated that to me with real life popping up to drag me away from working in conjunction with spending a lot of time on fine tuning it caused some oversights in some places of the project. I got a sense for better understanding the time requirements of features to ensure I have a complete experience in good time before the deadline, allowing for extra features to be added that enhance the existing gameplay loop.
