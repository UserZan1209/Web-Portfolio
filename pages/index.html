Programming has been my biggest passion since high school. While I enjoy creating games I have a wide interest in all things software development, as being able to design and create new and interesting software is something I find very fulfilling. I started my journey into game development initially with art and graphic design and taught myself programming when trying to develop my first few game projects. I instantly fell in love with the practice and the endless amount of things to learn within the subject and then decided to specialize in the programming side of game development and went on to pursue a Bachelor of Science degree.
